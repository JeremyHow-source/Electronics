## 6. Core Services (One-Stop Analysis)

### 6.1 Material Analysis (MA) / Surface Analysis

Surface and interface characterization at microscopic/nanoscale levels. Provides high-sensitivity, high-precision quantitative analysis for defects at sub-micron and nanoscale.

**Testing Content:**
- Surface analysis of inorganic/organic materials, contaminants, residues
- Quantification of surface elemental composition
- Chemical status/bonding information determination
- Depth analysis
- Film/material and oxide thickness measurement
- Line scan and area mapping

**Surface Analysis Techniques:**
- TOF-SIMS (Time-of-Flight Secondary Ion Mass Spectrometry)
- D-SIMS (Dynamic Secondary Ion Mass Spectrometry)
- XPS (X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy)
- AES (Auger Electron Spectroscopy)
- FTIR (Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy)
- TXRF (Total Reflection X-ray Fluorescence)
- AFM (Atomic Force Microscopy)
- Nano-indentation
- Spectroscopic Ellipsometry
- XRD (X-ray Diffraction)
- TEM (Transmission Electron Microscopy)
- FE-SEM (Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscopy)
- EDX (Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectrometry)

**Key Equipment Details:**

**Atomic Force Microscope (AFM):**
- Depth resolution: minimum 0.1 nm
- Multiple application modules
- Vacuum-adsorbed sample stage for wafers up to 12 inches (non-destructive)
- Tap mode for flexible sample surfaces
- Applications: Surface imaging, roughness testing, nanoindentation hardness, microcurrent leakage detection

**X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy (XPS):**
- World's first scan-focused X-ray source (minimum 7.5 micron analysis area)
- Fully automated analysis with insulating sample neutralization
- High-performance depth analysis (75mm × 75mm stage)
- Optimal energy resolution: < 0.48 eV (Ag3d5/2)
- Signal source: 1–10 nm below surface
- Detection element range: Li to U
- Detection accuracy: 0.1–1.0 at%
- Ion sputtering depth: 1–500 nm
- Sputtering accuracy: 1–10 nm (Argon ions)

### 6.4 Reliability Analysis (RA)

Evaluates IC reliability by accelerating aging through increased temperature, humidity, bias voltage, simulated I/O, and other stress conditions.

**Test Methods:**

#### TC – Temperature Cycle Test
- Standard: JESD22-A104
- Purpose: Accelerate effects of temperature changes
- Temperature range: -70°C to +180°C
- Temperature slew rate: 15°C/min linear
- Chamber volume: 160L
- Samples: 25 / 3 lots
- Pass criteria: 700 cycles / 0 Fail; 2300 cycles / 0 Fail

#### BLT – High Temperature Bias Test
- Standard: JESD22-A108
- Purpose: Effect of high temperature bias on sample
- Temperature range: +20°C to +300°C
- Chamber volume: 448L
- Samples: 25 / 3 lots
- Pass criteria: Shell temperature 125°C, 1000 hrs / 0 Fail

#### HAST – Highly Accelerated Stress Test
- Standard: JESD22-A110/A118
- Purpose: Evaluate reliability in wet environments (moisture penetration through mold compound)
- Device 1: Temp -105°C to +142.9°C; Humidity 75–100% RH; Pressure 0.02–0.196 MPa; 51L
- Device 2: Temp -105°C to +142.9°C; Humidity 75–100% RH; Pressure 0.02–0.392 MPa; 180L
- Pass criteria: 130°C/85% RH/96 hrs/0 Fail; 110°C/85% RH/264 hrs/0 Fail

#### THB – Temperature & Humidity Bias Test
- Standard: JESD22-A101
- Temperature range: -40°C to +180°C
- Humidity range: 10–98% RH
- Temperature slew: 3°C/min
- Chamber volume: 784L
- Samples: 25 / 3 lots
- Pass criteria: 85°C/85% RH/1000 hrs/0 Fail

#### TSA & TSB – Temperature Shock Test
- Standard: JESD22-A106
- TSA: -70°C to +200°C; conversion within 5 min; 70L
- TSB: -65°C to +200°C; conversion < 20 sec; 4.5L

### 6.5 Decap (Package Opening) Services

Ten specialized decap techniques:

1. **Complex Product Decap** — Devices covered by silicone/plastic molding compound; uses mechanical, laser, chemical, grinding methods. For automotive modules, controllers, sensors, power devices.

2. **Decap Remove Ball** — Hot plate/soldering iron removal of tin balls from BGA. Enables FA or replanting for crossover experiments.

3. **Decap Cleaning Pin** — Chemical/wiping to clean contaminated pins after RA testing for ATE testing.

4. **Decap Ceramics/TO** — Mechanical pry of metal covers, no chemical treatment. Maintains original state for contaminant/delamination analysis.

5. **Die Extraction** — Chemical etching to remove chip. Retains AI Pad with special treatment. Works for front/back side FA, flip packages, III-V optoelectronic chips.

6. **Decap Plastic Au Wire** — Chemical opening retaining gold wire electrical connectivity. Retains 1st/2nd solder joints for EFA.

7. **Decap Plastic Others (Cu/Ag/Al wire)** — Laser + chemical for copper/silver/aluminum wire packages. Note: Cu/Ag wire may be corroded.

8. **Decap Die Special** — Special packaging requiring multiple corrosion/laser steps. Retains pads, enables Si and III-V chip extraction.

9. **Decap PCBA (Board-Level)** — Laser + acid for PCB boards. Achieves single/double-sided opening while preserving other positions.

10. **Decap Laser Processing** — Red laser slotting/thinning with controllable energy/depth. Reduces chemical exposure time for copper wire; retains EFA capability.

## 10. The "Labless" Concept

**Proposed by:** Li Xiao Min (end of 2020)  
**Definition:** The fourth operational model of the semiconductor industry — stripping the "necessary non-core" R&D/analysis link from the industry chain into an independent track.

**Context & Rationale:**
- Analogous to how "Fabless" separated chip design from manufacturing (led by TSMC/UMC in late 1980s)
- In-house labs (In-HouseLab) face: cost out of control, technology iteration ceiling, talent skill degradation, glass ceiling for analyst careers
- Independent third-party labs serve the whole industry with inherent cost/technology advantages
- Barriers to entry: Hardware investment (hundreds of millions of dollars), talent cultivation (5–10 years per qualified analyst), IP from R&D iteration, trust/neutrality

**Wintech's Neutrality Principle:**  
"Do not touch the equipment at the top, do not touch the products at the bottom" — focuses purely on testing and analysis without IP disputes with customers or suppliers.
